I am a software engineer that enjoys applying computational thinking
to a wide range of disciplines. My work experiences in college spanned
cybersecurity, high performance computing, and storage systems. After
college, I spent 3.5 years in the aerospace industry, learning new
concepts like orbital dynamics on the job. I also took initiative to
solve problems beyond what was asked such as addressing a major
storage issue facing the entire software team. I left aerospace to
focus on a year long solo project designing and building a living
space inside a van including an electrical system, plumbing system,
woodworking, and more.

Outside of work, I can be found traveling the country in my van
to enjoy the serene natural beauty that surrounds us. I enjoy all
manner of human powered recreation in the outdoors, my favorite 
being long runs in the mountains.
